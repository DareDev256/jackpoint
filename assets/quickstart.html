02 · the terminal

First, let's open the Terminal

All of your AI assistants live in one place: an app called Terminal. It's a plain little window where you type a word, press Enter, and the computer responds. That's the whole idea. It looks bare on purpose — it's fast, not fancy.

1

Hold ⌘ Command and tap Space. A search box pops up.

2

Type the word Terminal

3

Press Enter. A window opens with a blinking line.

your-mac ~ %

That blinking block is the cursor. It's just waiting for you to type something and press Enter. You'll use this same ⌘-Space trick to open any app on a Mac — you just learned a real shortcut.

07 · if it seems stuck

It's almost always easy

New things feel breakable. These almost never are. Three little hiccups people hit, and the fix for each.

◎

Nothing happens when I type?

Click inside the Terminal window first so it's listening, and check you pressed Enter. Still quiet? Open a fresh one with ⌘ N and try again.

⌕

It says "command not found"?

Usually a small typo, or setup is still finishing. Check the spelling, then fully quit Terminal with ⌘ Q, wait a minute, reopen, and try once more.

⌁

It mentions "no internet"?

The online ones (gemini, claude, codex) need wifi. Check you're connected — or switch to an offline one: type chat and keep working with no internet at all.

The universal fix: close Terminal, reopen it, try again.
You really can't break anything by typing.
